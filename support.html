Need help? Email comicbookhistorians@gmail.com. Say which iPhone or iPad you have and what you were doing when the problem happened. A screenshot helps a lot.

Common questions

Where are my projects saved?
On your device. Maximum has no account and no cloud storage. Deleting the app deletes its projects, so to keep a project safe or move it to another device, open it and save a project file (.twoframe.zip) from the project menu.
The app closed while I was editing.
Open it again. Maximum saves as you go and offers to pick up where you stopped.
Remove Background says my phone can’t do it.
The screen says why. The most common reasons are an iOS version older than 17 or a phone that is too hot. Let it cool for a few minutes, or update iOS in Settings, then try again.
Remove Background is slow.
Every frame of the clip is worked out on your phone. Older iPhones and Low Power Mode make this slower. Shorter clips finish sooner, and each clip only has to be prepared once.
My export has no sound.
Check that the clip’s volume is not at zero and that it is not muted on the timeline. If it still has no sound, email us and include the lines shown under “Sound details” on the export screen.
Where does my finished video go?
To your Photos library. The first time, iOS asks whether Maximum may add to your photos; tap Allow. If you said no, you can change it in Settings, Privacy & Security, Photos, Maximum.
Can I share my own effect with other people?
Yes. Build it in Effect Lab, then choose Submit. Effects are checked by a person before they join the community library, and the terms are shown before you send one.

Privacy

Your videos and photos stay on your device. Maximum does not collect personal data, has no ads and no tracking. Some tools download their model files the first time they are opened; the file host sees your device’s internet address, as any website does.
